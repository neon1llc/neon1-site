services

Workflow automation

Connect your systems, automate what crosses them, and build what's missing.

  • Inventory, orders, customers, or invoices disagree across systems and get reconciled by hand.
  • A vendor sync or script broke quietly and nobody owns it.
  • Reporting needs data your systems don't export cleanly.
  • Documents arrive in volume and need extraction with human review.

details

Cloud and AI engineering

Design, build, and improve cloud infrastructure, delivery, and AI applications, weighing reliability, security, and cost.

  • A launch or migration needs an independent review and a rollback plan.
  • An architecture or cost decision is coming, such as a renewal, a new region, or a platform choice.
  • Hand-built infrastructure should move to infrastructure as code.
  • An AI feature must answer from your data, respect who may see what, cite sources, and be evaluated first.

details

Networks, devices, and connected systems

Design and integrate networks, devices, and connected environments, weighing reliability, security, and visibility.

  • A flat network carries point-of-sale, cameras, guests, staff, and smart devices and should be segmented.
  • A second location or remote staff need consistent design and secure access.
  • Sensors, IoT devices, cameras, or building systems need hardware and software integrated and monitored.
  • A home network or lab needs a higher standard, with local storage and private recording.

details

Security readiness

Assess risks, strengthen controls, and prepare systems for security reviews and audits.

  • A customer or insurer sent a security questionnaire, and the answers need to be true.
  • A SOC 2, HIPAA, or PCI DSS review is coming, and controls or evidence aren't ready.
  • Access has grown loose, with shared accounts, missing MFA, unreviewed admin rights, or slow offboarding.
  • Backups exist, but a restore has never been tested.

about

I'm Pradeep Tathineni, and neon1 is my independent consulting practice, formed in July 2025.

Before neon1, I spent more than eight years as a cloud and DevOps engineer, mostly on AWS: migration, release automation, hardening, and monitoring, for government, financial services, media, and security organizations.

neon1 began with free work for people I already knew. It gave me room to practice and refine the methods I use today, and it keeps me close to the community I care about.

process

Listen

Goals, systems, people, constraints, and who will own the result.

Propose

A recommendation, or options when the choice is real, with scope, acceptance criteria, cost to build and run, and alternatives.

Decide

You approve the scope and price. Sometimes the answer is a setting, an existing product, or no change.

Build

Reviewable changes, tested against the acceptance criteria, including failure and rollback. You approve scope changes.

Hand over

Evidence it meets the criteria, documentation, and a walkthrough for whoever runs it. Ongoing care is optional.

In 1NEON, you manage and oversee each project through needs, execution, observation, and next steps. A free account lets you outline up to three projects, chat with me, and request an assessment. See a walkthrough with fictional data.

questions

Can I start without a call?

Yes. Start a 1NEON account, outline up to three projects on your own time, then ask for an assessment of any or all of them. I read them and reply by email, and you can message me in the chat from your account.

How do you treat our data and accounts?

I use only the access the job needs, keep your information confidential, and remove my access when we're done. We sign a short service agreement once, before any paid work.

What if something urgent comes up?

I work in planned appointments, and a care plan sets my response times in writing. An outage or incident in progress needs a provider with emergency coverage first. I take it from there, with the cause, the fix, and what to change.

How is the cost worked out?

After we talk, I send a short proposal: one recommendation, or options when there's a real choice, each with what it covers, its upkeep, and an estimate. Discovery sets the estimate. Unforeseen work or purchases are agreed with you before I add them.

What if a project needs a specialist?

When a project needs a licensed installer, an independent auditor, or a penetration tester, I bring in a vetted one and write their role into the proposal.
