services

01 · for small to medium businesses

Business workflows and technology. Email, spreadsheets, POS, booking, and accounting connected, with the repetitive steps automated, so nobody retypes orders at the end of the day.

02 · for technical teams

Cloud and AI engineering. Infrastructure as code, deploy pipelines with checks and rollback, AI assistants that answer from your own documents, and backups proven by test restores.

03 · for offices, homes, and physical spaces

Networks, devices, and spaces. Wi-Fi that reaches the rooms you use, cameras that record and alert as set, devices configured and documented, and smart gear on its own network.

04 · for organizations, teams, families, and individuals

Security awareness and readiness. Plain-language sessions on phishing, fraud, passwords, and data brokers, plus SOC 2 readiness for companies heading into an audit.

The first call is free and takes 15 minutes. If a small fix is enough, I'll say so.

process

Listen

Show me how the work gets done today. One real example is enough.

Propose

A few options, from the quickest fix to the fuller build, each with an estimate, the effort, and what it gets you.

Decide

You pick. Sometimes that means a new system. Often it's a setting, a cleanup, or leaving it alone.

Build

Small changes, tested, and shown to you as they land.

Hand over

I show you how it works and write it down, so you can run it without me, then check that it does what we agreed. Ongoing care is optional.

See a sample project in 1NEON, start to finish, with made-up data, or try the demo.

about

I'm Pradeep. For about 15 years I've kept a strong curiosity and passion for technology and automation, and put them to work at home and on the job. neon1 is how I do that work for local businesses, teams, and families, so they end up better off with their tech than before.

I listen before I build, and you get your options even if that's all you need. I keep it as simple as the problem allows, explain costs up front, and test that the work does what we agreed. Your accounts, data, and documentation stay yours.

questions

What if something urgent comes up?

I work in planned appointments on weekday evenings and weekends, and I don't offer 24/7 or emergency response. Urgent issues, such as a security incident in progress or an outage you can't run without, need a provider with emergency coverage. Ongoing care after a project is agreed in writing, including any response times. Email hello@neon1.tech for anything else.

How is the cost worked out?

After the first call I send a short proposal with options, usually a quick fix, a solid setup, and a fuller build. Each comes with a cost estimate and says what it covers and how much effort it takes, how much is automated, what upkeep it needs, and how well it copes when something fails. Every option meets the same baseline for security and quality; a smaller option does less, not worse. You pick one. If the scope or the work changes along the way, I'll tell you before it changes the cost. See how options look in 1NEON.

How do you treat our data and accounts?

Before any paid work, we sign a short services agreement once. It covers how I'll handle your systems and information: only the access the job needs, kept confidential, and handed back or removed when we're done. After that, each project is a proposal with options, and choosing one, on paper or in 1NEON, is what we both agree to. It works both ways, so you'll know what I need from you and why. If the work touches personal or health data, the right agreement, such as a Business Associate Agreement, comes first. See a handover in 1NEON.

Do you run security training?

Yes, for groups from parents and teachers to engineers and executives. Sessions cover the threats people meet most, such as phishing, fraud, and data brokers, plus accounts and passwords, home and business networks, cameras and smart devices, and physical security. For organizations I also cover privacy policies, data handling, security frameworks, and incident response.

What don't you do?

I don't repair electronics, though I can debug software and hardware installs and get them working together. I don't handle personal or health data without the right agreements in place, such as a Business Associate Agreement, but I can help you get them. I'm not a lawyer, but I'll work alongside yours on the technical side.
