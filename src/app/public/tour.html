Maple Street Bakery: orders without retyping

This is what a small project looks like in 1NEON, the portal every client gets. A two-person bakery takes orders by email and phone, then retypes them into a spreadsheet each night. Follow it through the four layers: Needs, Execute, Observe, and Normalize.

1 · Needs2 · Execute3 · Observe4 · Normalize

step 1

Needs

Request

Currently: every night we copy about 40 orders from email and voicemail notes into a spreadsheet. It takes an hour, and a few orders get missed each week.

Goal: orders land in one list on their own, and nobody retypes them.

Timing: before the holiday rush.

Options

Every proposal offers a few choices, each with its cost, effort, and what it gets you. The figures below are illustrative.

  • Quick fix · an order form that writes straight into the spreadsheet · about 4 hours
  • Solid setup · the form, plus emailed orders parsed into the same list, with a daily summary · about 16 hours chosen
  • Fuller build · all of that, plus a small order page with pickup times and reminders · about 40 hours

Decision

The owner picked Solid setup in the portal on Oct 3. The decision, who made it, and the estimate are kept with the project.

step 2

Execute

Work

  • Order form connected to the spreadsheet
  • Emailed orders read into the same list
  • Daily summary at 7pm
  • Two weeks running side by side with the old way

Update · Oct 17

Health: on track. Emailed orders now arrive in the list within a minute. One order with a photo attachment needed a manual fix; that case is handled now. Waiting on you: confirm the summary should go to both owners.

step 3

Observe

Measures

step 4

Normalize

Handover

  • A one-page runbook: where orders go, and what to do if one looks wrong
  • A 30-minute walkthrough with both owners, recorded for new staff
  • Every account and bill in the bakery's own name

Care

Optional and monthly: a check that the numbers above still hold, and small changes as the bakery grows. The bakery can also run it without neon1.
