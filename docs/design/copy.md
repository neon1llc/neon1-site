# Copy

The words on every neon1 surface. The full voice is the corpus [writing standard](https://github.com/neon1llc/neon1-corpus); this page is the screen-sized version.

## Rule

Least and best words for understanding, interest, and confidence. Judge every line: if removing it loses nothing, remove it.

## Cut on sight

- Meta and obvious mechanics: "sends at once", "this is saved", "click the button below".
- Repeated information, in the same view or across views.
- Intro and outro help text. A control is clear by its label, or the control is wrong.
- Disclaimers outside their one place. A caveat lives once, in the policy, the terms, or the corpus FAQ, and other places carry only the substance.
- Selling words, intensifiers, soft selling, and absolutes that are not literally true.
- Dashes and semicolons in prose.

## Write

- Lead with the action or the fact. Label what the control does: "Send invite", not "Submit".
- Sentence case for titles, buttons, and headings. Numerals for 10 and up and for money, measurements, and versions.
- `neon1` is always lowercase, even starting a sentence. `neon1 LLC` only in the copyright line and legal text. Say "free" rarely: once, where it names something.
- Errors: what happened, then how to fix it. No blame, no "oops".
- Empty states: what belongs here, then the one action.
- Links say where they go. Never "click here".
- Helper text is never larger than the title above it.

## Voice

First person where the company speaks, because neon1 is one person's practice. Plain, specific, candid about limits. Say what neon1 does not do when someone else fits better.

## Check

Read the screen as a first-time visitor on a phone. Cut a third. Check each remaining line for a fact, an action, or a reason to trust. Check names, numbers, and dates against the corpus.
