services

01 · for small to medium businesses

Business workflows and technology. Email, spreadsheets, POS, booking, and accounting connected and automated, so nobody retypes orders at night.

02 · for technical teams

Cloud and AI engineering. Infrastructure as code, deploys with checks and rollback, AI that answers from your own documents, and backups proven by restores.

Networks, devices, and spaces. Wi-Fi that reaches every room, cameras that record and alert, documented devices, and smart gear on its own network.

Security awareness and readiness. Plain-language sessions on phishing, fraud, passwords, and data brokers, plus SOC 2 readiness before an audit.

The first 15-minute call is free. If a small fix is enough, I'll say so.

process

Listen

Show me how the work gets done today. One real example is enough.

Propose

Options from quickest fix to fuller build, each with an estimate, the effort, and the result.

Decide

You pick. Often it's a setting, a cleanup, or leaving it alone.

Build

Small changes, tested, and shown as they land.

Hand over

I check it does what we agreed, then show you and write it down, so you can run it without me. Ongoing care is optional.

See a sample project in 1NEON, with made-up data, or try the demo.

about

I'm Pradeep. For about 15 years I've put my passion for technology and automation to work at home and on the job. neon1 brings that to local businesses, teams, and families, so they're better off with their tech than before.

I listen before I build, and you get your options even if that's all you need. I keep it simple, explain costs up front, and test that the work does what we agreed. Your accounts, data, and documentation stay yours. The mission and promise puts it all on one page.

questions

What if something urgent comes up?

I work in planned appointments on weekday evenings and weekends, with no 24/7 or emergency response. A security incident in progress or an outage you can't run without needs a provider with emergency coverage. Ongoing care, and any response times, are agreed in writing. Email hello@neon1.tech for anything else.

How is the cost worked out?

After the first call I send a short proposal with options, usually a quick fix, a solid setup, and a fuller build. Each gives a cost estimate, what it covers, the effort, the upkeep, and how it copes with failure. Every option meets the same security and quality baseline; a smaller one does less, not worse. You pick. If the scope changes, I'll tell you before the cost does. See how options look in 1NEON.

How do you treat our data and accounts?

Before any paid work, we sign a short service agreement once, in 1NEON or on paper. It covers your systems and information: only the access the job needs, kept confidential, and removed when we're done. Each project is then a proposal with options, and choosing one, signed on paper or in 1NEON, is what we both agree to, including what I need from you. Work with personal or health data needs the right agreement first, such as a Business Associate Agreement. See a handover in 1NEON.

How do I pay?

Invoices come through 1NEON, in the project they belong to, and are due within 15 days. Each option in a proposal is priced one of two ways, and says which: a fixed price for work that's well understood, or an estimate, which is billed for the hours I work, lists each one, and never passes its high end unless you approve a change first. A project usually starts with a deposit (half of a fixed price, or half of an estimate's low end), and the rest is billed once you accept the handover. You can pay online by bank transfer (ACH) or card through Stripe, or by check, Zelle, or wire. There's no surcharge for any of them. If I buy equipment or software for you, you pay back exactly what the receipt shows, with no markup.

Do you run security training?

Yes, for groups from parents and teachers to engineers and executives. Sessions cover phishing, fraud, data brokers, passwords, networks, cameras, smart devices, and physical security. For organizations I add privacy policies, data handling, security frameworks, and incident response.

What don't you do?

I don't repair electronics, though I debug software and hardware installs and get them working together. I don't handle personal or health data without the right agreements, such as a Business Associate Agreement, but I can help you get them. I'm not a lawyer, but I'll work with yours on the technical side.
