White rabbit scene assets

Car model based on "2019 Chevrolet Camaro" by IsaacOldton (https://sketchfab.com/IsaacOldton),
licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified: unbranded,
recolored, stripped of hidden parts, and optimized.

Sunflowers based on "Sunflower" by zvanstone (https://sketchfab.com/zvanstone), licensed
CC BY 4.0. Modified: weathered, posed in a bed, and rendered as a picture.

Tomato plants based on "Tomato Plant" by zvanstone (https://sketchfab.com/zvanstone), and the long
squash on "Crookneck Squash" by vtIndigenousCommunityGarden
(https://sketchfab.com/vtIndigenousCommunityGarden), both licensed CC BY 4.0. Modified: posed in
beds and cages, stretched and recolored, and rendered as a picture.

Trees (tree_small_02, jacaranda_tree), grass clumps (grass_medium_01), dandelions (dandelion_01), and the leather, walnut, carpet, plaster, and floor
wood planks, and leafy grass textures are from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0.

The V8 recording is the site owner's own.
